I am a **Temporary Teaching and Research Fellow (ATER)** in Applied Mathematics at the **Université des Antilles** (Pointe-à-Pitre, Guadeloupe). 

I hold a Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics from the **University of Douala** (Cameroon). My research focuses on mathematical modeling, ordinary differential equations, and dynamical systems applied to biological, agricultural, and environmental problems.

## Research Interests

* **Agricultural & Ecological Systems:** Mathematical modeling of the impact of water stress, climate effects, and soil parameters on crop production (with a focus on Banana-Plantain systems).
* **Epidemiology & Public Health:** Immuno-epidemiological models and environmental epidemiology, studying the impact of fine particulate matter ($\text{PM}_{10}$ and $\text{PM}_{2.5}$) on cardiovascular and respiratory disease dynamics.
